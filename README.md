## Nadu Barbashova 

I'm PhD candidate in Psychological & Brain Sciences @ UCSB, advised by Dr. Jonathan Schooler and also collaborating with Dr. Hongbo Yu to understand the nature of emotion and cognition. 

At the moment analyzing the latent dimensions of fear, studying affective forecasting and moral injury.

I am interested in the cognitive aspects of fear. What we know abotu ourselves, the world and the possibility space in which we live can drive our experience of the world. Sometimes, an event may something shatters our model of the world because it goes beyond the bounds of our possibility space. At other times it may be that our possibility space is so expanded to include terrible events that we are always in expectation of a disaster that never arrives. The way that we form beliefs about the world, ourselves and morality interests me.
