# Nadu Barbashova 

I'm PhD candidate in Psychological & Brain Sciences @ UCSB, advised by Dr. Jonathan Schooler and also collaborating with Dr. Hongbo Yu to understand the nature of emotion and cognition. 

At the moment analyzing the latent dimensions of fear, studying affective forecasting and moral injury.

I am interested in the cognitive aspects of fear. What we know about ourselves, the world and the possibility space in which we live can drive our experience of life. 

Sometimes, an event may shatters our existing model of the world because it goes beyond the bounds of our envisioned possibility space. Something happens that we did not think was even possible. At other times it may be that our possibility space is so expanded to include extreme/rare events that we are always in expectation of them to arrive again, only to discover that most of our lives will take place in a world different than the one we've modelled in our minds. 

The way that we form beliefs about the world, ourselves and morality interests me - it is a constant updating, and in the process emotions guide us to simulate the future and to chart a course toward it.
